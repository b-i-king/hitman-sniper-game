SPOTTER // ONE SHOT

You lie in the grass with a pair of binoculars. Beside you, Ghost, an AI sniper, waits behind the rifle. He only shoots what you tell him to. Find the target through your binoculars, read the weather, and talk him onto the shot.

How to play

  1. Turn on your mic. Click MIC (or press M) for hands-free listening, or hold Space to talk and release to send. Allow microphone access. Use Chrome or Edge. Headphones help, so the mic doesn't hear Ghost. No mic? Type the same words in the box at the bottom.
  2. Read the briefing. It tells you who the target is (suit, tie, glasses, hat) and what the weather is doing. Say start mission.
  3. Look through your binoculars. Drag the view or use the arrow keys to scan, and scroll to zoom. Every person has a number tag. Press B to lower the binoculars and see Ghost lying next to you.
  4. Name the target. Target three or, radio style, Tango three.
  5. Talk like it's a radio net if you like. Numbers digit by digit work: Range four five zero, niner for 9. Ghost answers the same way.
  6. Check Ghost's answer. Ghost tells you what he sees through his scope ("black suit, red tie"). If that's not your man, pick again.
  7. Read the conditions on the right and call them:
    • Range: Range six hundred. In fog or heat haze the laser can't get a reading, so measure on the binocular scale instead. A person is about 1.75 m tall, so range = 1.75 × 1000 ÷ height in mils.
    • Wind: Wind eight from the right. It gusts. Watch the meter and the orange flags.
    • Temperature: Temperature fifteen. Cold air means the bullet drops more, hot air means it drops less.
    • Movement: Moving at one. Ghost aims ahead of him. Or wait until he stops.
  8. Moving target? Ghost keeps his crosshair locked on the person you named as he walks (or as the train moves). You only add the lead (Moving at one) and any nudges (a little left, two clicks up). Nudges stay relative to the target.
  9. Aim for the head: Go for the head. A body shot may not kill him.
  10. Give the order: Send it! On the train, say Fire when ready and Ghost shoots the moment the window is clear.
  11. If he survives, follow up. A miss or a wound sends him running and the civilians scatter. You have about 8 seconds: Moving at three, send it!

Modes

Survival (the main mode): endless contracts with new targets, ranges, weather, crowds and trains. Every contract is harder. Fail one and you lose a life, then retry the same contract (or say new contract to roll a different one). Three lives. How far can you go?

Training: five fixed drills, from an open field to a moving night train. Each one teaches a skill.
