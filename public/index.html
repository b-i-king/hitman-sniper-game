SPOTTER // ONE SHOT

A voice-controlled sniper game. You are the spotter. An AI sniper is on the rifle beside you and does exactly what you say. Find the target, read the conditions, call the shot.

🎧You · Spotter

Identify the target, read range, wind and temperature, and talk the sniper onto the shot.

🎯AI · Sniper

Dials the solution you call, tracks the target, reads back, and fires on your order.

🕴️The Target

Viktor "The Viper" Kovac. Don't let him escape. Don't hit civilians.

Missions

What can I say?
"Target three"Pick the person with that number tag
"Range six hundred"Distance in meters (gravity drop)
"Wind eight from the right"Crosswind in mph and where it comes from
"Temperature fifteen"°F: cold air = more drop, hot air = less
"Moving at four"Target speed (m/s): sniper leads him
"Go for the head" / "Center mass"Aim point
"Up 0.5" / "Left 1"Manual corrections in mils
"Send it" / "Fire"Take the shot now
"Fire when ready"Sniper fires as soon as the shot is clear
"Hold fire" · "Status" · "Reset"Cancel · Read back solution · Clear corrections
